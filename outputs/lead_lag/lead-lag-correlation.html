CHronoZ | Lead-lag correlation¶

Summary¶

The saved run evaluates 8,775 pair/lag combinations across 351 pairs, using calendar shifts from -12 to +12 months. 194 pairs have their largest absolute Pearson correlation at zero lag; 157 pairs peak at a nonzero lag. Every evaluated lag has at least 60 paired observations. These in-sample maxima are not predictive validation.

Charts and tables use full dataset titles. Missing months remain missing. This summary describes the saved run; the final summary cell recomputes the results when inputs or parameters change.

Context & Methods¶

How does pairwise association change when one monthly change series is shifted relative to another?

Assumptions and scope¶

  • Native monthly series only, selected by both expected and inferred frequency. Weekly and quarterly exclusions are displayed.
  • Use the same explicit percentage-change / first-difference registry as the initial correlation notebook. Rates use percentage-point changes; hours use hour changes.
  • Changes require consecutive calendar months. Dropped observations remain gaps; a September-November move is never treated as one month.
  • No re-cleaning, interpolation, filling, frequency conversion, model training, or automatic variable removal.
  • Correlation is descriptive co-movement. It does not establish causality or predictive value. No release-calendar/vintage data is available, so observation dates are not information-availability dates.
  • Define lag k as corr(X at t, Y at t+k). Positive k means the first variable precedes the second by k calendar months; negative k reverses that ordering.
  • Scan -12 to +12 calendar months using >=60 pairs per lag. Select the largest absolute Pearson value per unique pair; ties favor the smallest absolute lag.
  • Peak selection searches many comparisons and can overstate apparent strength. Different lag profiles can use different samples. Results are exploratory, not significance tests or out-of-sample forecasts.

Inputs: data/cleaned/series_metadata.csv, the referenced cleaned series, and data/audit/data_cleaning_audit.csv. All inputs are read directly; this notebook does not depend on another notebook being run. Outputs: outputs/lead_lag/. Full titles are used in reports; source IDs remain internal.
