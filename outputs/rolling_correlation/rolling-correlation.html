Context & Methods¶

How stable are contemporaneous monthly-change associations through time?

Assumptions and scope¶

  • Native monthly series only, selected by both expected and inferred frequency. Weekly and quarterly exclusions are displayed.
  • Use the same explicit percentage-change / first-difference registry as the initial correlation notebook. Rates use percentage-point changes; hours use hour changes.
  • Changes require consecutive calendar months. Dropped observations remain gaps; a September-November move is never treated as one month.
  • No re-cleaning, interpolation, filling, frequency conversion, model training, or automatic variable removal.
  • Correlation is descriptive co-movement. It does not establish causality or predictive value. No release-calendar/vintage data is available, so observation dates are not information-availability dates.
  • Pearson correlations use trailing 60-calendar-month windows, including the labeled end month, with >=48 paired observations. No future observations enter a window.
  • The calendar grid contains NaNs for absent months, never fabricated values. Windows begin only after 60 calendar months have elapsed since the first paired observation. Constant windows are undefined.
  • Adjacent windows overlap heavily; paths and observed ranges are descriptive and are not independent statistical tests. Four illustrative pairs are pre-specified rather than selected by their strongest full-sample correlations.

Inputs: data/cleaned/series_metadata.csv, the referenced cleaned series, and data/audit/data_cleaning_audit.csv.
